# Diagram attribution

The original per-task attribution fields are preserved in each frozen public index.

- Project-authored D/H/J/S diagrams retain their existing CC BY 4.0 declarations. They share renderer styles and fold families; they are not evidence of independent web/book generalization.
- D007 is a crop of step 1 of Tavin's traditional Sailboat diagram. Source: https://tavinsorigami.com/?mdocs-file=880 . The source displays a CC BY notice; the frozen index records CC BY 3.0. The crop, not the entire model, is the evaluated instruction.
- D008 is a crop of step 1 of Tavin's traditional Fox diagram. The historical source URL is https://tavinsorigami.com/diagram/Fox.pdf ; the current download is https://tavinsorigami.com/?mdocs-file=856 . The source states CC BY 3.0: https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/ .
- Original source pages for the external-document tasks are not redistributed. Their source URLs, PDF hashes and rendered-pixel hashes are recorded in `data/external-qualified/source-manifest.json`; the preparation tool obtains matching files locally.
- Native solver and viewer dependencies retain their upstream license files. See `native/NOTICE.md` and the bundled dependency sources. Data attribution does not replace those code licenses.

Source notices were checked on 2026-11-02. No third-party book pages are included in this release.
